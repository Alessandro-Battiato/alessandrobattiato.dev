# Personal Portfolio

## Overview

My personal developer portfolio built as a client-side React application. The portfolio highlights my experience, technical skills, selected work, and approach to building software through a focused, interactive interface with engaging 3D elements.

## Goals

1. Present my professional profile and experience clearly.
2. Showcase the selected case study with enough technical context to demonstrate engineering depth.
3. Communicate a product-oriented approach to software development.
4. Demonstrate proficiency in full-stack product development, modern web technologies, product-oriented engineering, and interactive 3D.
5. Provide an engaging visual experience without sacrificing usability or performance.
6. Make the portfolio responsive and accessible across desktop and mobile devices.

## Core User Flow

1. Visitor lands on the portfolio.
2. Visitor gets an immediate overview of the developer and their role.
3. Visitor explores the main sections through navigation or scrolling.
4. Visitor reviews experience, skills, and selected projects.
5. Visitor explores the case study's technical details.
6. Visitor can interact with selected UI and 3D elements.
7. Visitor reaches the contact section and can follow the available professional links.

## Features

### Hero and Positioning

- Product Engineer positioning presented immediately above the fold.
- Concise introduction focused on building and shipping digital products.
- Interactive React Three Fiber visual as the primary visual element.
- Clear navigation to the main portfolio sections.

### Engineering Approach

- Dedicated section describing the developer's approach across Product, Engineering, Analytics, and AI.
- Emphasis on the connection between technical implementation and product outcomes.
- Content structured to communicate how problems are approached rather than only listing technologies.

### Featured Case Study

- Detailed presentation of a selected product or project.
- Case study structured around the full product lifecycle:
  - Product
  - Research
  - Ideation
  - Product Strategy
  - Design
  - Engineering
  - Launch
  - Analytics
  - Iteration
- Technical implementation and architectural decisions presented alongside product context.
- Product analytics and iteration included where relevant.

### Professional Experience

- Selected professional experience presented through concise, outcome-oriented descriptions.
- Focus on responsibilities, technical contributions, and product impact.
- Current experience highlighted as evidence of real-world product development.

### Current Focus

- "Now" section showing current areas of development and learning.
- Product Analytics
- Product Engineering
- Experiment Design
- Selected technologies, concepts, or certifications can be surfaced as supporting evidence.

### Contact

- Direct access to LinkedIn, GitHub, and email.
- Minimal contact experience with no authentication or form backend required.

### Interactive Experience

- React Three Fiber and Three.js used for selected visual interactions.
- Animations enhance the experience without interfering with content consumption.
- Responsive and accessible fallback behavior for reduced-motion or unsupported WebGL environments.

## Scope

### In Scope

- Single-page personal portfolio.
- Product Engineer positioning and personal introduction.
- Responsive navigation and section structure.
- Interactive hero experience built with React Three Fiber.
- Product and engineering approach section.
- One detailed featured case study.
- Professional experience section.
- Current focus / "Now" section.
- Contact and professional links.
- Subtle UI animations and micro-interactions.
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Accessibility and reduced-motion considerations.
- Client-side performance optimization.
- Static asset and 3D model optimization.
- Static production deployment through Netlify.

### Out Of Scope

- Authentication and user accounts.
- Backend APIs or server-side application logic.
- Database or persistent application data.
- CMS or admin dashboard.
- Contact form backend or message persistence.
- User-generated content.
- Real-time features or collaboration.
- AI-powered functionality inside the portfolio.
- E-commerce, payments, or subscriptions.
- Dynamic project management or content editing.
- Mobile-native applications.

## Success Criteria

1. Visitors can quickly understand the developer's Product Engineer positioning and full-stack product development focus.
2. The featured case study demonstrates the connection between product thinking, engineering decisions, delivery, analytics, and iteration.
3. The portfolio maintains a consistent visual identity across light and dark themes.
4. The interface remains responsive, accessible, and usable across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
5. Interactive 3D and animations enhance the experience without compromising content readability or performance.
6. The application builds as a static client-side site and deploys successfully to Netlify without requiring backend infrastructure.
